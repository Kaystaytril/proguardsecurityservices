Armed response

Tactical vehicles, live GPS tracking and a control room that never closes.

Fast because we are local

Proguard has a reliable fleet of tactical response vehicles, manned by efficient, highly trained response officers. All of our reaction officers are PSIRA members and have been through intensive in-house training.


Because our response is area-based, we get to each call-out quickly and resolve your urgent request for help. Each vehicle is branded, tracked live and in constant contact with our control room, so it responds according to our policy and your instructions.
